# CopyKat Agent Marketing Kit

Marketing skills for real estate agents, from CopyKat Consulting.

## Add it to Claude

You need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).

1. In Claude, open **Customize → Plugins**.
2. Choose **Add → Add marketplace → Add from a repository**.
3. Enter: `copykat-consulting/agent-marketing-kit`
4. Find **CopyKat Agent Marketing Kit** in the list and click **Add**.

If skills don't run, check that code execution is on under **Settings → Capabilities**.

## What's inside

| Skill | What it does | How to start |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing Command Center** | Builds your own private marketing dashboard: monthly content checklist with draft and go-live dates, review and approval workflow, social and Google Business performance from Metricool, content ideas board and a 12-month year plan. | "Set up my Marketing Command Center" |
| **Brand Brain Builder** | Interviews you in 5 short steps (brand assets, voice, audience and offer, visuals, compliance) and builds your Brand Brain, so everything Claude writes sounds like you and follows your brand. | "Help me build my Brand Brain" |
| **Real Estate Newsletter Setup** | Builds your monthly email newsletter as HTML ready to paste into Mailchimp or Constant Contact: hero, video spotlight, articles and a listings grid in your brand. | "Set up my monthly newsletter" |
| **Market Report Blog** | Turns your local association or MLS monthly stats into a branded market report blog post, ready to paste into your website. | "Write this month's market report" (paste or upload the stats) |
| **Brand Voice Editor** | Runs a draft (your monthly community or lifestyle blog, market report, newsletter or email) through your Brand Brain so it sounds like you, keeping every fact and stat intact. | "Run this blog through my Brand Brain" (paste or upload the draft) |
| **Monthly Social Plan** | Turns your month's blogs, market report and newsletter into the full social plan on your Command Center checklist: posting calendar, captions, video and reel scripts, B-roll shot lists, Google Business posts and designed feed posts. Marks each item ready for review on your dashboard and, once you approve, loads drafts into Metricool. | "Build my social plan for November" (after your blogs and newsletter are done) |
| **Single-Topic Email** | Turns your lifestyle blog (or your market report, if you prefer) into a short one-topic email in your newsletter's design, ready to paste into Mailchimp. | "Make my single-topic email from this blog" |
| **Market Report Postcard** | Builds a print-ready 8.5 × 5.5 postcard from your monthly market report: headline stats, a community table, your headshot, branding and compliance. | "Make my market report postcard" |
| **Monthly Marketing** | Your guide for the whole month. Walks you through each piece in order (market report, lifestyle blog, newsletter, single-topic email, postcard, then social media and videos) and marks each one Ready for review on your Command Center as you go. | "I'm ready to create my marketing for November" |

**Tip:** start with the Brand Brain. The Brand Voice Editor and the other writing skills use it to match your voice.

**Each month:** say "I'm ready to create my marketing for <month>" in your Claude Project. The Monthly Marketing guide walks you through every piece in order and tracks it all on your Command Center. Bring your lifestyle blog (and its video script, if you have one) and your association's monthly stats.

For live social numbers in the Command Center, first connect your Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile in Metricool, then connect **Metricool Social Media Management** in Claude under Settings → Connectors.
