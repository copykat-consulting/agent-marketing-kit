# CopyKat Agent Marketing Kit

A month of real estate marketing, built and tracked in Claude. From CopyKat Consulting.

## Add it to Claude

You need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).

1. In Claude, open **Customize → Plugins**.
2. Choose **Add → Add marketplace → Add from a repository**.
3. Enter: `copykat-consulting/agent-marketing-kit`
4. Find **CopyKat Agent Marketing Kit** in the list and click **Add**.

If skills don't run, check that code execution is on under **Settings → Capabilities**.

## Three steps

Do all three inside one Claude Project for your marketing (for example "Jane Smith Marketing"). Everything you make is saved there, and each step reads what the one before it saved.

| Step | What happens | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Build your Brand Brain** | A 5-part interview (brand assets, voice, audience and offer, visuals, compliance) that becomes your Brand Brain: the reference Claude uses so everything sounds like you and follows your brand and brokerage rules. Add your logos and headshot to the Project too. | "Build my Brand Brain" |
| **2. Set up your Marketing Command Center** | Your private dashboard, pre-filled from your Brand Brain: the monthly content checklist with draft and go-live dates, a review and approval queue, the Social plan tab, social and Google Business numbers from Metricool, a content ideas board and a 12-month year plan. | "Set up my Marketing Command Center" |
| **3. Create your monthly marketing** | Every month. Claude opens your dashboard and walks you through each piece in order, pausing for your review after each one and marking it Ready for review on your dashboard. | "I'm ready to create my marketing for November" |

### What Step 3 makes each month

| # | Piece | What you bring |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market report blog | Your association's monthly stats (PDF or numbers) |
| 2 | Lifestyle blog, edited into your voice and coded to match your market report post, plus its video script | Your blog text and video script |
| 3 | Monthly email newsletter (Mailchimp-ready HTML) | Live blog links, if they're up |
| 4 | Single-topic email from your lifestyle blog (or the market report) | |
| 5 | Market report postcard, 8.5 × 5.5, print-ready PDF | Headshot and a front photo, the first time |
| 6 | Social media and videos: the posting calendar, captions, designed feed posts and carousels, Google Business posts, video and reel scripts, B-roll shot lists | Photos for the month |
| 7 | Metricool drafts, after you approve | Your designs in a Google Drive folder |

You can stop after any piece and pick up later: your dashboard shows where you left off.

## The skills behind it

Steps 1–3 use these skills. You can also run any of them on its own.

| Skill | What it does | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Brain Builder** | Step 1. | "Build my Brand Brain" |
| **Marketing Command Center** | Step 2. Also updates older dashboards with new features. | "Set up my Marketing Command Center" |
| **Monthly Marketing** | Step 3: the monthly guide. | "I'm ready to create my marketing for <month>" |
| **Market Report Blog** | Turns your association's monthly stats into a branded blog post for your website. | "Write this month's market report" |
| **Lifestyle Blog** | Edits the blog you bring into your voice and builds it as HTML in the same design as your market report post. | "Format my lifestyle blog" (paste the blog) |
| **Brand Voice Editor** | Runs any draft through your Brand Brain so it sounds like you, keeping every fact. | "Run this blog through my Brand Brain" |
| **Real Estate Newsletter Setup** | Your monthly email newsletter as HTML for Mailchimp or Constant Contact. | "Build my newsletter" |
| **Single-Topic Email** | One blog post turned into a short email in your newsletter's design. | "Make my single-topic email from this blog" |
| **Market Report Postcard** | A print-ready 8.5 × 5.5 postcard from your market report. | "Make my market report postcard" |
| **Monthly Social Plan** | Your social plan for the month, designed posts, video scripts, and Metricool drafts once you approve. | "Build my social plan for November" |

## Connections

- **Metricool** (for live social numbers on your dashboard and for loading drafts): connect your Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile in Metricool, then connect **Metricool Social Media Management** in Claude under Settings → Connectors.
- **Google Drive** in Metricool (so it can pull your post images): link it once in your Metricool account settings.
