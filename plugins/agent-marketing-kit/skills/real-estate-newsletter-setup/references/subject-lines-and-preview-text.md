# Subject Lines & Preview Text

Guidance for any client's monthly newsletter — generalized from patterns
that worked well in practice.

## Subject line approaches worth offering
Give the client 3-5 options across a few different angles rather than one
guess, so they can pick or A/B test:
- **Seasonal/topical hook** — mirrors the hero headline for cohesion.
- **Market-forward** — leads with the market update for recipients who
  primarily read this for local market intel.
- **Curiosity/video hook** — leads with whatever the video spotlight
  covers; curiosity-driven subject lines often outperform on open rate.
- **Lifestyle-forward** — leads with the local-life content pillar.
- **Short & understated** — a few words, lower-key, for clients whose brand
  voice leans more restrained than promotional.

Keep every option under ~45 characters so it doesn't truncate on mobile
inboxes, where most opens typically happen.

## Preview text
- This is the snippet shown next to/under the subject line before someone
  opens the email — distinct from the email's actual opening line. Don't
  let it just repeat the hero paragraph's first sentence, or it reads
  redundant once the email is open.
- Should tease 2-3 concrete things inside the issue (e.g. "Market update,
  wine bars worth the drive, and a few weekends worth planning") rather
  than a vague tagline.

## When asked for subject lines
Always also ask (or infer from brand-quick-reference.md) whether the client
wants to lead with market data, lifestyle content, or the video — that
answer changes which of the above approaches to prioritize.
