# From checklist to deliverables

## Default plan items this skill covers

These are the Command Center's default keys. Agents can rename, resize or
remove them, and keys don't always match titles (the default `feed-posts`
key is titled "Carousel posts", while an agent's own `feed-posts` may be
plain feed posts). Always go by each item's title and quantity on the
month's checklist; this table only says what the default titles mean.

Common custom titles: "Short videos" usually means 15–30 second talking
tips (confirm with the agent; it could also mean B-roll), "Listing reel" is
a reel for a specific listing, "Open house post" is a feed post.

| Key | Default | Make | Source it from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `market-video` | 1, day 12 | Market update video: talking points, shot list, on-screen text, caption | The month's market report |
| `tour-video` | 1, day 22 | Blog video: the lifestyle blog told on camera, with talking points that follow its sections, shot list, on-screen text, caption pointing to the post | The lifestyle blog (the one in the newsletter or pasted with the request) |
| `reel-market` | 1, day 14 | 20–45 second reel script cut from the market video, plus caption and cover-frame text | Market video script |
| `reel-community` | 1, day 24 | 20–45 second reel script cut from the blog video, plus caption and cover-frame text | Blog video script |
| `broll` | 4, day 30 | Four B-roll clips: shot list, on-screen text line, caption each | Community blog places, the agent's niche, the month's theme |
| `feed-posts-15` | 15, day 30 | 15 single-image feed posts: design plus caption | Mix below |
| `feed-posts` (Carousel posts) | 4, day 30 | Four carousels of 5–8 slides: designs plus caption | One per major piece: market report, each blog, newsletter's tip or checklist feature |
| `market-graphic` | 1, day 12 | Market stats graphic: one design plus caption | Market report's headline numbers |
| `gbp-posts` | 4, day 30 | Four Google Business posts: text, image choice, button | Blogs and market report, one per week |
| `gbp-reviews`, `review-ask` | | Skip. Offer a short review-request text the agent can send | |

Not covered (other skills make these): `newsletter`, `single-topic-email`,
`listings-email`, `market-blog`, `community-blog`, `postcard`, and any
other Email, Blog or Direct mail item.

## Items the agent added

Read the title and channel and make the closest match: "Listing reel"
becomes a reel script for that listing (ask for its details and photos;
never use library photos for it), "Open house post" becomes a feed post,
"Testimonial post" needs a real review the agent supplies. An item with
`fromIdea` builds on that saved idea. If the title is unclear, ask in the
same round as other missing inputs.

## Feed post mix (for 15; scale for other counts)

| Type | Count | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Market stat | 3 | One headline number per community from the market report |
| Blog teaser | 3 | One sharp takeaway per blog post, pointing to the post |
| Expertise tip | 3 | The agent's niche checklist (horseable land, first-time steps, downsizing) |
| Local and lifestyle | 2 | A place or seasonal moment from the community blog or theme |
| Personal | 2 | From the Brand Brain's life outside work, within its rules |
| Invitation | 1 | The standard CTA, plainly |
| Relationship | 1 | Client-for-life touch: vendor list, client event, home checklist |

Every post maps to one source. If the month's content can't support a
type, swap in another type rather than inventing material.

## Calendar rules

1. Read every item's go-live day. Each deliverable goes live on or before
   it; counted items spread across the month and finish by that day.
2. Anchor dates first: market report go-live, then market video, market
   graphic and market reel within the following week; community blog
   go-live, then community video and reel after it.
3. Spread feed posts at roughly three to four a week. Never two of the
   same type back to back, and never two posts on one day unless the count
   requires it.
4. Carousels: one a week, on a different day from that week's video.
5. Google Business posts: one a week, each linking to the newest relevant
   blog post.
6. B-roll: one a week, on lighter days.
7. Place year-plan hooks (holidays, seasonal moments) on or just before
   their date. Skip any the Brand Brain excludes.
8. Times: what the agent names; else Metricool's best time for that
   network when connected; else 9:30 a.m. in the agent's time zone.

Number posts in calendar order (Post 1, Post 2, …) and use the same
numbers on the plan page, the design canvas and exported file names, so
everything matches up at scheduling time.
