# Post design brief

Build every designed post on one Claude Design canvas, following the Design
artifact type's own instructions for files and layout. If the agent has a
design system marked default, it wins wherever it differs from this brief.
Otherwise take colors, fonts and logo rules from the Brand Brain's Visual
Identity.

## Look

Quiet, editorial and photo-led, like a magazine spread rather than a
flyer. No badges, starbursts, price bursts, emoji, stock icons or busy
graphics. Lots of breathing room.

## Every post

- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5 portrait) for feed posts, carousel slides and
  the market stats graphic.
- **Photo:** full-bleed, filling the frame, from the agent's approved
  library or photos they supplied. Favor the images the Brand Brain says
  match their focus. Listing posts use that listing's own photos only.
  Each photo is uploaded to the canvas, never hot-linked or embedded inline.
- **Overlay:** a soft dark layer so white text reads cleanly: the agent's
  darkest brand color (or near-black) at about 30–45%, or a gradient that
  is darker only where the text sits. Adjust per photo; the image should
  still look bright and rich, never muddy.
- **Text:** white or the brand's lightest neutral.
  - Label (eyebrow): small, all caps, wide letter spacing (about 0.25em),
    in the body font. E.g. "FALLBROOK · NOVEMBER 2026".
  - Headline: the brand's headline font, large, sentence case, eight words
    or fewer. Never the caption. No phone numbers, prices or hashtags.
  - Optional hairline rule between label and headline, in a light accent.
  - Text sits on calm areas of the photo (sky, wall, open land), never over
    a face, an animal's face or the main subject. Keep at least 80 px from
    every edge.
- **Logo:** the agent's mark in white or light, small (about 80–110 px
  wide), like a signature. Recolor a dark logo to white; never stretch,
  outline, retype or redraw it. If no hosted logo exists, leave a
  labelled placeholder and list it.
- **Contrast:** check white text against the overlaid photo at 4.5:1 for
  labels and 3:1 for large headlines; darken the overlay where it fails.

## Rotating layouts

Use three or four and rotate them so the grid looks varied but cohesive.
Never put the same layout on two neighboring posts.

| Layout | Text | Logo |
|---|---|---|
| A. Low left | Label and headline stacked, left-aligned, bottom third | Top right |
| B. Centered low | Label and headline centered in the lower third | Top center |
| C. High left | Label and headline in the top third, over sky or open space | Bottom right |
| D. Framed | Thin light inset border about 40 px in; headline centered low | Bottom center |

## Carousels

- Slide 1 (cover): the photo treatment above, with the carousel's title.
- Content slides: either the same photo treatment with one idea per slide,
  or a solid slide in the brand's lead color with light text. Pick one per
  carousel and keep it consistent. Number slides small in a corner (2/6).
- Last slide: the agent's standard CTA, their logo and their contact
  method from the Brand Brain. Compliance lines go here if the brokerage
  requires them on graphics.

## Market stats graphic

Three or four headline numbers, each with a short label, plus the month
and source in small type at the bottom (e.g. "Source: Greater San Diego
Association of REALTORS®, October 2026"). A darker overlay or a solid
lead-color background so the numbers read clearly. Numbers exactly as the
market report has them.

## Canvas layout

- One artboard per post or slide, named with its post number and short
  title ("Post 04, Horseable checklist"). Carousel slides sit in one row
  per carousel.
- Rows grouped by week in posting order, with a title note for each week
  ("Week 1, Nov 2–8").
- Optional reel cover frames at 1080 × 1920 in a final row.
- Name each artboard file by post number (`P07`, and `P15-1`, `P15-2`, …
  for carousel slides). Step 8's render script names the images from these,
  so they match the plan. The agent doesn't need to export anything.
