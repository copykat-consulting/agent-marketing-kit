# Video, reel, B-roll and caption formats

Agents film themselves, often on a phone, and most don't want to read a
script word for word. Write talking points they can say naturally in their
own voice, with a few lines written out where wording matters (the hook,
any statistic, the close). Keep sentences short and spoken. Follow the
Brand Brain's video rules (for example, no dancing or trend videos, no
kids' faces).

## Long video (market update or community / lifestyle)

Target 90 seconds to 3 minutes.

```
TITLE: <working title>
LENGTH: about 2 minutes
WHERE TO FILM: <location; for a market video, a calm spot in one of their communities; for community, the places in the blog>
WHAT TO WEAR / BRING: <only if useful, e.g. "riding boots, the barn in the background">

HOOK (say this word for word, first 3 seconds):
"<one line that names the payoff>"

TALKING POINTS:
1. <point> | On screen: "<short text>"
2. <point, with the exact stat and source> | On screen: "<number + label>"
3. <point>
4. <what it means for buyers / sellers / their niche>

CLOSE (word for word):
"<their standard CTA as a calm invitation>"

SHOT LIST:
- A-roll: agent to camera, eye level, <setting>
- B-roll: <3-6 cutaways: places, details, aerials from the blog>

CAPTION: <full caption, see below>
```

## Reel cut-down

20–45 seconds, cut from the long video's footage where possible.

```
REEL: <title>  |  CUT FROM: <long video>  |  LENGTH: about 30 seconds
COVER TEXT: "<headline of 6 words or fewer>"

0-2s   HOOK: <line or visual>              On screen: "<text>"
2-10s  BEAT 1: <point>                     On screen: "<text>"
10-20s BEAT 2: <point>                     On screen: "<text>"
20-28s BEAT 3: <point>                     On screen: "<text>"
28-32s CLOSE: <invitation>                 On screen: "<CTA text>"

AUDIO: their voice; light, calm instrumental under it if any
CAPTION: <full caption>
```

## B-roll clip

7–15 seconds, no talking, text on screen.

```
B-ROLL <n>: <subject>
FILM: <what, where, time of day for good light>
SHOTS: <2-4 shots: wide, detail, slow pan or walk-through; 3-5 seconds each, steady>
ON SCREEN: "<one line, 8 words or fewer>"
CAPTION: <short caption, 1-3 sentences + CTA + sign-off>
```

Good B-roll for real estate: front-door walk-in, morning light on a porch
or patio, a local landmark or trail from the blog, coffee and a laptop
with the market report, hands on a gate or fence, a slow drive down a
neighborhood street, seasonal details.

## Captions (feed posts, carousels, videos, reels)

```
<Strong first line: the point, a number or a question. It shows before "more".>

<2-5 short paragraphs. One idea each. The agent's voice.>

<Their standard CTA as a calm invitation, with their contact method.>

Source: <publisher, month year>          (only when a statistic appears)

#<3-6 local and niche hashtags>

<Sign-off block exactly as in the Brand Brain>
```

- Instagram and Facebook can share one caption. LinkedIn, if on their
  plan: drop the hashtags to two or three and lead with the market insight.
- Use links only on Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business posts. On
  Instagram, say "link in bio."

## Google Business Profile post

```
GBP POST <n>: <title>
IMAGE: <which design or library photo>
TEXT (100-300 words, no hashtags, no phone number in the text):
<local, useful, keyword-natural: community names, what the post covers>
BUTTON: Learn more → <blog post URL>   (or Call)
```

## Review request (offer when review items are on the checklist)

Two or three warm sentences the agent can text or email to a recent
client, in their voice, with a placeholder for their Google review link.
No incentives and no pressure.
